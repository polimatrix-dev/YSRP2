About

The Youths Revolutionary Socialist Party was formed in 2026 to unite leftists and further actual leftist ideals in the United States of America.

We intend to help communities, advocate for workers' rights, form a revolutionary party, and ultimately unite against imperialism and capitalism.

Principles

  • Workers' rightsWe advocate for workers' rights, protections and better working conditions.
  • Community solidarityWe help communities through collective action and mutual assistance.
  • Leftist unityWe unite leftists around shared political goals.
  • Anti-imperialismWe oppose imperialism and support the self-determination of peoples.
  • Socialist idealsWe promote socialist ideals and challenge capitalism.

Organization

The Chairman

The highest executive authority of the party. The Chairman has authority over the Party Parliament and oversees party administration and political direction.

The Party Parliament

The representative body of the party. It deliberates on party matters and passes party measures. The Chairman is selected by the Parliament.

Check on the Chairman

A two-thirds vote of the Parliament can limit the Chairman's authority, and can veto and block actions and measures the Chairman wants to take or pass.

Members

Members are the foundation of the party and take part in its development and internal affairs.
